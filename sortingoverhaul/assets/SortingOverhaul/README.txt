Sorting Overhaul icons
Replace these PNGs to customize the pickers, then restart the game.
Keep filenames unchanged. Transparent square RGBA PNGs are recommended.
The supplied defaults are 128 x 128 pixels. The selected choice also appears in the header.
School-All.png is the scroll icon that opens the school picker when no school is selected.
Your customized files should be backed up before installing a later build.

The six school icons and Powers use native symbols, cleanly upscaled and lightly sharpened without layer effects.
Regenerating procedural placeholders will not recreate this customized artwork.

Weapon-WarAxe.png, Weapon-Battleaxe.png, Weapon-Mace.png, Weapon-Warhammer.png,
Weapon-Other.png. Every new placeholder is an exact Weapon-Blunt.png duplicate.
